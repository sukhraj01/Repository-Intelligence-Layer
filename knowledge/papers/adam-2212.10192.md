# ADAM — Tao, Liu, Shen, Xu, Geng, Jiao and Jiang (arXiv 2212.10192v2, June 2024), "Dense Retrieval Distillation with Adaptive Dark Examples"

Re-read 2026-11-14 from the stored PDF: body and appendices read page by page; the reference list (pp. 9 to 13) was checked for the entries the text cites rather than read entry by entry.

Source file: arXiv PDF v2 (6 Jun 2024), 13 pages (body pp.1-9, references pp.9-13, no appendix). Text passage retrieval distilled from a cross-encoder teacher; included for one design: a self-paced filter that keeps a shrinking, teacher-confident share of each batch.

## What it established

- Distilling a cross-encoder's score distribution into a dual encoder is limited because the teacher is nearly certain on mined hard negatives (their scores lie far below the positive's, Figure 1, p.1); adding "dark examples" (negatives concatenated with the positive; positives with random tokens masked) gives the teacher a smoother distribution to transfer (Sec. 3.2, pp.3-4).
- A self-paced rule: the teacher's confidence for a training instance is the log-probability of the positive against its negatives (Eq. 7); in epoch t of T, the instances of each batch are sorted by confidence and only the top share (1 - t/(2T)) enter the distillation term (Eq. 8, p.5). The kept share therefore shrinks linearly from the whole batch to one half over training.
- MS-MARCO dev MRR@10 with one teacher: random negatives 38.1, mined hard negatives 40.0, the full method 41.0; with a second teacher 38.1, 39.1 and 39.8 (Table 1, p.6). BEIR zero-shot average 42.8 against 42.0 for a curriculum distillation baseline (Table 2, p.8).
- Ablation on MS-MARCO MRR@10 (Table 3, p.8): full 38.99; without the strengthened negatives 38.82; without the masked positives 38.76; without both 38.64; without both and without the self-paced selection 38.61; additionally without the supervised term 38.36.

## What it did not establish

- The self-paced selection's own contribution is small and unreplicated: removing it after the dark examples are already removed changes 38.64 to 38.61 (Table 3, p.8; the table does not isolate it from the full method). No seeds, no intervals; single runs throughout.
- The confidence comes from a separate, stronger cross-encoder, not the model being trained; whether an own-model confidence behaves the same is not tested.
- Only text, only in distillation (soft labels), not a hard contrastive loss with labelled positives; MS-MARCO's positives are sparse labels that the authors themselves treat as possibly noisy.
- No matched-budget comparison: the kept share changes how many instances enter the distillation term per epoch, and the number of candidates per query changes with dark examples (limitation i, p.9).

## Method details we need

- Confidence C(q) = log of the softmax probability of the positive among positive and negatives under the teacher (Eq. 7, p.5); selection per batch and per epoch by the ratio (1 - t/(2T)), highest confidence kept (Eq. 8, p.5); loss = lambda times the supervised contrastive term on the whole batch plus the KL distillation term on the kept subset and the dark examples (Eq. 9, p.6).
- Candidates per query for distillation: the 10 original negatives, 10 concatenated negatives and 5 masked positives with masking ratios from 0.15 to 0.55 (Sec. 4.3, p.7).
- Teachers: two cross-encoder rankers; the student starts from a trained first-stage retriever (Sec. 4.3, p.7).

## Relation to RQ1 / RQ2 / RQ3

- RQ1, RQ3: none.
- RQ2: a worked example of "keep the examples the teacher is most sure of, and keep fewer as training goes on", the opposite direction of an easy-to-hard curriculum (which would show more examples later) and of a fixed K filter (same share every pass). It uses an independent, stronger teacher, which is the project's rule for K-rank, and a schedule for the kept share that the project's FILTER does not have. The effect attributable to the schedule is small and not isolated. It does not support ranking by teacher confidence as an established gain.

## Discrepancies

- With the master document: not cited. With CL-DRD (`cl-drd-2204.01458.md`): that paper orders pairs by teacher difficulty and grows the set; this one drops by teacher confidence and shrinks the set; both are called "curriculum" or "self-paced" in the text.
- Internal: the body calls the selected instances "high-confidence" and argues they are "far from the decision boundaries"; the same instances are said to have the sharpest teacher labels, i.e. the least dark knowledge (Sec. 3.3, p.5). The paper's argument keeps both claims; they pull in opposite directions.
